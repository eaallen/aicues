The problem

Chat history is a terrible filing cabinet.

The useful sentences live in the same place as the throwaway ones. There is no “keep this.” There is only “maybe I can find it later.”

Notes apps are not much better — they do not open ChatGPT for you. Copy, paste, switch tabs, hope you grabbed the right version.

How this started

I wrote a prompt I did not want to lose.

I needed AI to answer like a person, not a cheerful assistant. Shorter. Human. Still accurate.

Keep your responses short and to the point. Act more like a normal human than a chatbot. I want responses to feel human, with human-like mistakes in grammar and spelling, but keep the content spot on and correct.

Then I pictured it vanishing into chat history. So I built a place to keep it.

What it is

A wallet for prompts you like.

Save a cue once. Click it. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or another tool opens with the words already filled in.

That is the whole product. Store the prompt. Launch the chat. Stop digging.

How it works

Four steps. Then you are done.

Save a prompt → Pick an AI → Launch → Chat opens ready
